River Rafting

About This Adventure

Punakha's twin rivers, the Mo Chhu ("Mother River") and Pho Chhu ("Father River"), meet directly beneath Punakha Dzong, one of the most photographed buildings in the kingdom — and together they form Bhutan's premier river rafting destination. The Mo Chhu offers a gentler, scenic float past the dzong, willow groves and riverside farmland, ideal for first-timers, families and anyone who wants the thrill of white water without the intensity. The Pho Chhu, by contrast, carries faster, more technical Grade III rapids through a narrower gorge, popular with paddlers who've rafted before or simply want a bigger adrenaline hit.

Every trip opens with a full safety briefing and paddling lesson before you launch, so no prior rafting experience is assumed. Life jackets, helmets and paddles are provided, a certified guide steers each raft, and a safety kayaker follows the group throughout — you leave with both the thrill of the rapids and a proper feel for the valley, its dzong, its suspension bridges, and the slower rhythm of life beside the water.

Best run September to November or March to April for the clearest water and weather, though the gentler Mo Chhu can often be rafted year-round outside the peak monsoon months.
