Tour Overview

The Tiger's Nest Pilgrimage is a compact, deeply immersive introduction to Bhutan — built around the cliffside hike to Paro Taktsang, the monastery where Guru Rinpoche is said to have meditated in the 8th century. Beyond the hike itself, this tour weaves in the dzongs, temples and everyday life of Paro and Thimphu, guided throughout by a certified private cultural guide who brings each site's history and significance to life.

Tour Highlights

  • Hike to the cliffside Tiger's Nest Monastery (Taktsang)
  • A private blessing ceremony with a resident monk
  • Visit Rinpung Dzong and the National Museum of Bhutan
  • Explore Thimphu's Memorial Chorten and weekend market
  • A traditional farmhouse lunch with a local family
  • Guided throughout by a certified private cultural guide

Day By Day Itinerary

Land at Paro's dramatic mountain airport and settle into your hotel. In the afternoon, visit Rinpung Dzong and stroll through Paro town before an orientation dinner with your guide.

An early start for the cliffside climb to Paro Taktsang through pine and rhododendron forest, ending with a private blessing ceremony inside the monastery with a resident monk.

Drive to the capital, stopping at the National Museum en route. Afternoon exploration of Thimphu's Memorial Chorten, Buddha Point and the weekend handicraft market.

A full day with your guide across Thimphu's textile museum, takin sanctuary and weaving workshops, followed by a traditional farmhouse lunch with a local family.

A relaxed morning before your private transfer back to Paro International Airport for your onward flight.
