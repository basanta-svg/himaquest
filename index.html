We Do More Than Travel

We Sponsor A Child's Education

Education is one of the most powerful tools for lasting change. For every guest who travels with us, we sponsor a full year of school fees, books and uniforms for a child in a rural Bhutanese community. What begins as your journey through the Himalayas becomes an investment in someone else's future — helping break down the barriers that keep young minds from reaching their full potential.

And Support A Remote Monastery

Bhutan's remote monasteries are guardians of a living spiritual tradition, often surviving on modest local support alone. With every trip, we contribute directly to the upkeep and wellbeing of a remote private monastery — funding repairs, daily provisions and care for the monks who preserve centuries of Buddhist teaching in some of the kingdom's most isolated valleys. It's our way of ensuring the culture you come to experience continues to thrive for generations to come.
