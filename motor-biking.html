Motor Biking

About This Adventure

Few ways of seeing Bhutan match the feeling of leaning into a hairpin bend on a classic Royal Enfield, engine humming, prayer flags streaming past on either side. Our motorcycle tours follow the kingdom's dramatic highway network — from the pine-forested climb out of Paro, over Dochula Pass with its 108 memorial chortens, down into the subtropical warmth of Punakha, and on toward the high central valleys — putting you closer to the road, the weather and the mountains than any vehicle ever could.

Rides are guided and fully supported, with a support vehicle carrying luggage, spares and a mechanic following the group, so you can ride light and focus on the road. Enfield Classics and Himalayans are maintained to a high standard and fitted out for mountain touring, and routes are paced to suit the riders — social, unhurried mornings with plenty of stops for tea, photographs and roadside dzongs, rather than a race to cover distance.

A valid motorcycle licence and prior riding experience are required; routes and daily distances are tailored to your group's comfort and skill level, from a short two-day loop out of Paro and Thimphu to a full multi-day traverse across central Bhutan's high passes.
