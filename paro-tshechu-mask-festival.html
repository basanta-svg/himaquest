Tour Overview

The Paro Tshechu is one of Bhutan’s most important religious festivals, held annually in the courtyard of Rinpung Dzong. Over several days, monks and lay performers dance in elaborate costumes and painted masks, re-enacting stories from Buddhist scripture and the life of Guru Rinpoche, who is credited with bringing Buddhism to Bhutan in the 8th century.

The festival’s climax comes before dawn on its final day, when a massive thongdrel — an appliqued silk tapestry several storeys tall — is unveiled at Rinpung Dzong. Bhutanese believe simply viewing the thongdrel cleanses sin, and locals gather from across the region in the pre-dawn dark to receive its blessing.

This tour builds a full trip around the festival, adding a hike to Tiger’s Nest Monastery and a day in Thimphu, so travellers experience both Bhutan’s most vivid living tradition and its everyday cultural landmarks. Exact festival dates shift each year with the lunar calendar, so itineraries are timed individually to match.

Tour Highlights

  • Sacred masked cham dances performed in the courtyard of Rinpung Dzong
  • The pre-dawn unveiling of a giant thongdrel on the festival’s final day
  • A hike to the cliffside Tiger’s Nest Monastery (Taktsang)
  • Rinpung Dzong and Paro town’s traditional architecture
  • Thimphu sightseeing, including Tashichho Dzong and Memorial Chorten
  • Guided throughout by a certified private cultural guide

Day By Day Itinerary

Land at Paro and visit Rinpung Dzong, where the Tshechu is held, for an orientation ahead of the festival.

A full day at the festival grounds, watching sacred masked cham dances performed by monks and lay dancers.

An early start before dawn to witness the unveiling of the giant thongdrel, followed by a relaxed afternoon in Paro.

A cliffside climb through pine forest to Paro Taktsang (Tiger’s Nest Monastery), with a blessing ceremony inside.

Drive to the capital, visiting Tashichho Dzong, Memorial Chorten and the Buddha Dordenma statue.

A relaxed morning before your private transfer back to Paro International Airport for your onward flight.
