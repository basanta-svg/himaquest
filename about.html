About Us

About Us

Locally Rooted Creators of Remarkable Himalayan Journeys

Himaquest Travels was founded in Thimphu with a simple belief — that Bhutan is best experienced slowly, guided by people who call these mountains home. What began as a small team of local guides and planners has grown into a fully licensed tour operator trusted by travellers from across the world, without ever losing that personal, ground-level care.

Every itinerary we design is built from scratch around the traveller in front of us, not pulled from a shelf of fixed packages. Our guides are certified, our partner hotels and drivers are chosen for reliability and warmth, and our payments are held in a government-regulated account so every trip is as secure as it is unforgettable.

More than two decades on, our purpose remains unchanged: to open the Kingdom of Bhutan to the world with honesty, care, and a deep respect for the culture that makes it extraordinary.
