The Five-Lodge Valley Circuit Overview

The Five-Lodge Valley Circuit is Himaquest's signature leisure journey through Bhutan — a lodge-to-lodge route across five of the country's most distinctive valleys, each with its own character, altitude and view. Rather than moving through Bhutan hotel to hotel, this circuit is built around a small selection of premium and boutique lodges chosen specifically for their setting and service, so every stop is a destination in its own right.

Travelling by private vehicle with your own guide throughout, the route moves from the pine-forested heights of Paro, through the capital Thimphu, down into the subtropical warmth of the Punakha valley, up to the glacial cirque of Gangtey and finally east into the wide, sacred Bumthang valley. Each lodge stay includes gentle guided walks, valley excursions and cultural visits suited to a relaxed pace, with time built in simply to enjoy each property.

This is a fully private, fully customisable journey — no fixed group, no trekking or camping required, and every detail from room category to daily activities shaped around how you want to travel.

Day By Day Itinerary

Land at Paro's mountain-ringed airport and settle into your first lodge, with the afternoon free to explore the valley or simply unwind after your flight.

A guided hike to Tiger's Nest Monastery in the morning, then an easy afternoon wandering Paro's old town and riverside farmland.

Drive to Thimphu, Bhutan's capital, checking into your lodge with time to visit the city's landmarks — Buddha Dordenma, the weekend market and Tashichho Dzong.

Cross Dochula Pass, with its 108 memorial chortens and, on a clear day, Himalayan views, before descending into the warm Punakha valley and visiting Punakha Dzong.

A gentle walk across Punakha's suspension bridge and rice terraces, with an optional visit to a local farmhouse before returning to the lodge.

Climb into the Black Mountains to the glacial cirque of Gangtey (Phobjikha), winter home of the endangered black-necked crane, and check into your lodge overlooking the valley floor.

Continue east into Bumthang, Bhutan's spiritual heartland, visiting Jakar Dzong and Jambay Lhakhang en route to your final lodge.

A relaxed day exploring Bumthang's sacred sites and rural villages, with time at the lodge to enjoy the valley before your final night.

Fly out from Bumthang's domestic airport, or drive back toward Paro for your international departure, depending on your onward plans.

What Makes This Circuit Different

Rather than a standard hotel-to-hotel itinerary, every stop on this circuit is chosen for its own sense of place — a lodge worth staying at, not just sleeping in. Rooms, dining and daily pace are arranged around comfort and view rather than a fixed sightseeing checklist, with your guide free to adjust each day depending on weather, energy levels and what catches your interest along the way.
