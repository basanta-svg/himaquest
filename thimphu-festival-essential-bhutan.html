Overview: Thimphu's Biggest Festival, Bhutan's Best Introduction

Every autumn, Tashichho Dzong's courtyard in Thimphu fills for three days with masked dancers, monks and thousands of Bhutanese pilgrims dressed in their finest clothes for Thimphu Tshechu — the capital's largest and most attended religious festival. It is Bhutan's festival calendar at its most vivid: cham dances performed to the sound of long horns and cymbals, families picnicking on the dzong lawns, and a genuine sense of national celebration.

Because hotel space in Thimphu is limited during festival week, this departure is run as a confirmed set trip with rooms secured in advance, paired with the country's essential sightseeing — the hike to Tiger's Nest Monastery above Paro, and Thimphu's dzong, memorial chorten and weekend market.

It is an ideal introduction to Bhutan for first-time visitors who want to see the country's biggest festival without piecing together the logistics themselves.

Trip Highlights

  • Three days of masked cham dances at Thimphu Tshechu in Tashichho Dzong
  • Hike to Taktshang (Tiger's Nest) Monastery above the Paro valley
  • Visit Thimphu's memorial chorten and the Buddha Dordenma statue
  • Browse Thimphu's weekend market alongside local shoppers
  • Guided walk through Paro town and its riverside dzong
  • Pre-booked hotel rooms for festival week, when the capital is at its busiest

Day By Day Itinerary

Arrive at Paro International Airport, settle into your hotel and take a gentle walk through Paro town before a welcome dinner.

A half-day hike to Taktshang Monastery, followed by a visit to Paro Dzong and the National Museum.

Drive to Thimphu and spend the afternoon at the capital's memorial chorten and Buddha Dordenma statue before the festival begins.

Three days attending Thimphu Tshechu at Tashichho Dzong, watching the masked dances and joining the festival crowds, with time for the weekend market and further capital sightseeing between sessions.

Drive back to Paro for your private transfer to the airport for your onward flight.
