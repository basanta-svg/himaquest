The Amba Collection Overview

The Amba Collection is a boutique route through three of Bhutan's most visited valleys, built around Amba's small, design-led hotels rather than large-format luxury properties. It opens and closes at Amba Paro, a hotel known for pairing contemporary Bhutanese architecture with uninterrupted valley views, and moves through Thimphu and Punakha in between at a relaxed, unhurried pace.

Travelling by private vehicle with your own guide throughout, the route balances Paro's iconic sights with the capital's everyday rhythm and Punakha's warm, low-altitude valley — each stop chosen for refined comfort and a genuine sense of place rather than scale.

This is a fully private, fully customisable journey — every detail from room category to daily activities shaped around how you want to travel, with the option to extend into Gangtey or Bumthang for a longer trip.

Day By Day Itinerary

Land at Paro's mountain-ringed airport and settle into Amba Paro, with the afternoon free to explore the hotel grounds and valley views.

A guided hike to Tiger's Nest Monastery, Bhutan's most iconic sight, followed by an easy afternoon back at the hotel.

A gentle day exploring Paro's old town, riverside farmland and Rinpung Dzong at an unhurried pace.

Drive to Thimphu, Bhutan's capital, checking into a boutique hotel with time to visit Buddha Dordenma and the weekend market.

Cross Dochula Pass, with its 108 memorial chortens and, on a clear day, Himalayan views, before descending into the Punakha valley and visiting Punakha Dzong.

A gentle walk across Punakha's suspension bridge and rice terraces, with an optional visit to a local farmhouse before returning to the hotel.

Drive back over Dochula Pass to Paro for a final night at Amba Paro, with the evening free to relax before departure.

Transfer to Paro International Airport for your onward flight.

What Makes This Trip Different

Rather than moving through Bhutan hotel to hotel at scale, this trip is built around a small selection of boutique properties chosen for design and atmosphere over size. Rooms, dining and daily pace are arranged around comfort and view rather than a fixed sightseeing checklist, with your guide free to adjust each day depending on weather, energy levels and what catches your interest.
