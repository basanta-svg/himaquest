Tour Overview

Bumthang is widely considered the spiritual heartland of Bhutan, a collection of four valleys — Choekhor, Tang, Ura and Chumey — holding some of the country’s oldest and most sacred temples. This pilgrimage travels overland from Paro through Punakha and Trongsa, crossing the Pele La and Yotong La passes before reaching Bumthang itself.

At the centre of the pilgrimage is Jambay Lhakhang, one of 108 temples said to have been built in a single night in 659 CE by the Tibetan king Songtsen Gampo, and Kurjey Lhakhang, where Guru Rinpoche is believed to have left a body imprint in rock while subduing a local deity. Tamshing Lhakhang and the sacred lake of Membartsho, the "Burning Lake" linked to the treasure-revealer Pema Lingpa, round out the pilgrimage circuit.

A day trip to the remote Ura valley, with its cobblestone streets and traditional cluster housing, offers a glimpse of rural Bhutanese life largely unchanged for generations. Throughout, a certified private cultural guide provides context for the region’s deep religious significance.

Tour Highlights

  • Jambay Lhakhang, one of Bhutan’s oldest temples, dating to the 7th century
  • Kurjey Lhakhang, site of Guru Rinpoche’s body imprint in rock
  • Tamshing Lhakhang, founded by treasure-revealer Pema Lingpa
  • Membartsho, the sacred "Burning Lake"
  • Trongsa Dzong, ancestral seat of the Wangchuck royal dynasty
  • A day trip to the traditional village of Ura

Day By Day Itinerary

Land at Paro and settle in, with an orientation walk through Paro town.

Cross Dochula Pass and its 108 chortens, then visit Punakha Dzong and Chimi Lhakhang.

Drive over the Pele La pass to Trongsa, visiting Trongsa Dzong and its Ta Dzong heritage museum.

Cross the Yotong La pass into the Bumthang valley, visiting Jakar Dzong on arrival.

A full day visiting Jambay Lhakhang, Kurjey Lhakhang, Tamshing Lhakhang and the sacred Membartsho.

Drive to the remote Ura valley, exploring its traditional cluster village and temple before returning to Bumthang.

Fly or drive back toward Paro for your onward transfer and flight.
