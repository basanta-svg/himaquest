Overview: A Battle Re-Enacted, A Festival That Follows

In the grounds around Punakha Dzong each winter, local men take up period armour and traditional weapons to re-enact the Punakha Drubchen — a dramatisation of a 17th-century battle in which Bhutanese forces repelled an invading Tibetan army. It is one of Bhutan's more unusual festival events: a staged militia battle rather than a monastic dance, performed with real intensity by local participants rather than professional performers.

The Drubchen runs directly into Punakha Tshechu, the valley's more conventional religious festival of masked cham dances, so this departure covers both events back to back, at the height of Punakha's mild winter climate. Around the festival days, the itinerary takes in Punakha Dzong itself, the drive over Dochula Pass with its 108 memorial chortens, and a day in Thimphu.

Run as a small group of up to 10 travellers, it's a compact, sociable way to see one of Bhutan's more distinctive festival pairings.

Trip Highlights

  • Watch the Punakha Drubchen, a re-enacted 17th-century battle in period armour
  • Stay on for Punakha Tshechu's masked cham dances immediately after
  • Visit Punakha Dzong, built at the confluence of the Pho Chhu and Mo Chhu rivers
  • Cross Dochula Pass and its 108 memorial chortens
  • A day of sightseeing in Thimphu, the capital
  • A small group of up to 10 travellers, guided throughout

Day By Day Itinerary

Arrive at Paro International Airport and drive over Dochula Pass to Punakha, with a stop at the pass's 108 chortens.

Visit Punakha Dzong and take a short walk through rice terraces to Chimi Lhakhang before the festival begins.

Watch the Punakha Drubchen battle re-enactment staged around the dzong grounds, with local militia in period armour recreating the 17th-century conflict.

Two days attending Punakha Tshechu's masked cham dances, held immediately after the Drubchen concludes.

Drive back over Dochula Pass to Thimphu, with an afternoon of capital sightseeing.

Continue sightseeing in Thimphu before an afternoon drive to Paro for a relaxed evening.

A relaxed morning before your transfer to Paro International Airport for your onward flight.
