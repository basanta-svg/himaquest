Jomolhari Loop Overview

The Jomolhari Loop is an extended version of the classic Jomolhari Trek, continuing past base camp over Nyile La to the remote Lingshi valley before looping south through Shodu and Barshong to finish in Thimphu, rather than retracing the same trail back to Paro. It is a full traverse through Jigme Dorji National Park, taking in higher passes and quieter, less-trodden trail than the standard out-and-back route.

Beyond the same close-up views of Mount Jomolhari (7,314m) that make the classic trek so popular, the Loop adds the dramatic Lingshi Dzong, perched above its valley, and a stretch of trail seen by far fewer trekkers each year. Blue sheep are frequently spotted on the descent toward Barshong, and the final days pass through increasingly dense forest as the route drops toward Thimphu.

This is a fully supported camping trek — a dedicated crew of guides, cooks and pack animals travels with you, pitching camp and preparing meals at each stop along the way.

What To Pack For The Jomolhari Loop Trek

Nights at Jangothang and Lingshi can drop well below freezing even in peak season, so pack a warm sleeping bag rated to at least -10°C, a down jacket, waterproof shell, thermal base layers, sturdy broken-in hiking boots, trekking poles, a headlamp, sun protection and a basic personal first-aid kit. Your trek crew carries tents, kitchen equipment and food, so you’ll only need a daypack for the walking day itself.
