Bumdra Trek Overview

The Bumdra Trek is the shortest and most accessible trek we offer — a single overnight above Paro that pairs a genuine camping experience with an unforgettable arrival at Bhutan’s most iconic site, Tiger’s Nest (Taktsang) Monastery. It is a favourite for travellers who want a taste of trekking without committing several days to it.

The trail climbs from the Sang Choekhor side of the Paro valley through pine and juniper forest to Bumdrak Monastery, where camp is pitched at 3,800m with sweeping views over the entire Paro valley — a spectacular spot to watch the sunset and, the next morning, the sunrise.

The second day’s descent is the highlight: a gentle walk down through forest past Zangto Pelri Lhakhang, arriving at Taktsang Monastery from above — a rare approach most visitors never see — before the final descent to the valley floor.

Day By Day Itinerary

Drive to the trailhead near Sang Choekhor and trek roughly 4 hours through pine and juniper forest to camp beside Bumdrak Monastery, with sweeping views over the Paro valley at sunset.

An early descent through forest past Zangto Pelri Lhakhang, arriving at Taktsang (Tiger’s Nest) Monastery from above. Visit the monastery before the final descent to the Paro valley floor.

What To Pack For The Bumdra Trek

Even for a short trek, nights at Bumdrak camp can be cold, so bring a warm layer or light sleeping bag liner if using rented gear, a waterproof shell, sturdy walking shoes, a headlamp, sun protection and water. Your trek crew carries tents, kitchen equipment and food, so a small daypack is all you need for the walk itself.
