Gangtey Lodge Valley Retreat Overview

Gangtey Lodge sits on a wooded ridge above the wide, glacial Phobjikha valley, its glass-walled suites and farmhouse-inspired architecture designed to frame the valley floor below rather than compete with it. This retreat builds a full circuit around it, moving through Paro, Thimphu and Punakha before finishing at Gangtey for the final two nights.

Travelling by private vehicle with your own guide throughout, the route balances Paro's iconic sights and Punakha's warm valley with the slower, wilder rhythm of Phobjikha — home to the endangered black-necked crane and some of Bhutan's most open, unbroken views.

This is a fully private, fully customisable journey — the number of nights at each stop, room category and daily activities can all be shaped around how you want to travel, with the option to extend into Bumthang for a longer trip.

Retreat Highlights

  • Two nights at Gangtey Lodge, overlooking the glacial Phobjikha valley
  • Glass-walled suites and farmhouse-inspired luxury architecture
  • Winter home of the endangered black-necked crane
  • Punakha Dzong and the warm, low-altitude Punakha valley
  • A private guide and vehicle for the entire circuit, door to door
  • Gentle valley walks paced entirely around you, no trekking required

Day By Day Itinerary

Land at Paro's mountain-ringed airport and check into your hotel, with the afternoon free to explore the valley or simply unwind after your flight.

A guided hike to Tiger's Nest Monastery, then an easy afternoon wandering Paro's old town and riverside farmland.

Drive to Thimphu, Bhutan's capital, with time to visit Buddha Dordenma, the weekend market and Tashichho Dzong.

Cross Dochula Pass, with its 108 memorial chortens and, on a clear day, Himalayan views, before descending into the Punakha valley and visiting Punakha Dzong.

A gentle walk across Punakha's suspension bridge and rice terraces, with an optional visit to a local farmhouse.

Climb into the Black Mountains to the glacial cirque of Gangtey (Phobjikha) and check into Gangtey Lodge, set on the ridge above the valley floor.

A gentle walk along the valley floor and a visit to the Black-Necked Crane Information Centre (seasonal), with the rest of the day free at the lodge.

Drive back to Paro for your international departure, with a stop en route depending on flight timing.

What Makes This Retreat Different

Rather than ending on another town or dzong, this circuit closes with two unhurried nights at one of Bhutan's most striking lodges — a valley with no through-road, wide open skies and, for much of the year, resident cranes. Your guide adjusts each day depending on weather, energy levels and what catches your interest along the way.
