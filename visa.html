Bhutan Visa Guide

Everything you need to know about entering Bhutan in 2026 — fees, the Sustainable Development Fee, required documents and how the online application actually works.

Visa Overview

Bhutan Runs A Fully Online Visa System

Every foreign visitor to Bhutan needs a visa before arrival, with the exception of nationals of India, Bangladesh and the Maldives, who travel on a simpler entry permit instead. There is no visa-on-arrival for most nationalities, so the entire process — application, document upload and payment — happens online through the Department of Immigration's official portal, either by yourself or through a licensed Bhutanese tour operator such as Himaquest Travels, who can apply on your behalf. Approval typically takes about five working days, and the visa is issued only once your Sustainable Development Fee (SDF) has been paid in full.

Fees

Visa Fee & Sustainable Development Fee (SDF)

Alongside the one-time visa application fee, every visitor pays Bhutan's Sustainable Development Fee for each night of their stay — the rate below is confirmed through 31 August 2027. The SDF funds free healthcare and education, environmental conservation and infrastructure across the kingdom, and is collected as part of the same online application.

US$40 Visa application fee — one-time, non-refundable
US$100 SDF per adult (age 12+), per night
US$50 SDF per child (age 6–11), per night
Free SDF for children under 6
INR 1,200 SDF per night for Indian, Bangladeshi & Maldivian nationals
Step By Step

How To Apply For A Bhutan Visa

Step 1

Create An Account & Sign The Pledge

Register on the Department of Immigration's official visa portal (or let Himaquest Travels apply on your behalf) and digitally sign Bhutan's Pledge of Friendship, a short commitment to responsible, respectful travel in the kingdom.

Step 2

Enter Your Travel Details

Add your passport information, arrival and departure dates, and the places you plan to visit. Upload a digital copy of your passport's bio page and a recent passport-style photo.

Step 3

Pay The Visa Fee & SDF

Pay the US$40 visa application fee together with the Sustainable Development Fee for every night of your stay, by credit card or bank transfer through the portal.

Step 4

Receive Your Visa Clearance

Applications are typically reviewed within five working days. Once approved, you'll receive a visa clearance letter by email — print a copy or save it on your phone to show at check-in and on arrival in Bhutan.

Before You Apply

Documents You'll Need

  • A passport valid for at least 6 months beyond your departure date from Bhutan
  • A digital scan of your passport's bio-data page
  • A recent digital passport-style photograph
  • Confirmed arrival and departure dates, and your flight or entry-point details
  • Valid travel insurance for the duration of your trip — not compulsory for every nationality, but strongly recommended and sometimes requested during processing
Travelling From India, Bangladesh Or The Maldives?

You Don't Need A Visa

Nationals of India, Bangladesh and the Maldives don't apply for a visa at all — instead, you apply online in advance for a simple entry permit, still paying the SDF at the reduced regional rate of INR 1,200 per person, per night. Indian nationals may also arrange the permit in person at the border, though applying online beforehand avoids any wait.

Note: Regardless of nationality, we recommend applying at least 2–3 weeks before departure to allow time for processing and any follow-up requests.
