Cycling

About This Adventure

Bhutan's near-empty back roads, quiet farm tracks and steadily climbing mountain highways make it one of Asia's most rewarding places to ride. Routes wind through terraced rice paddies and pine forest, past chortens and prayer wheels turned by passing streams, and through villages where a passing cyclist is still enough of a novelty to draw a wave from every farmhouse porch. Whether you're after a gentle valley-floor ride or a proper climb over a 3,000m+ pass, the terrain and the traffic-free roads reward riders of very different fitness levels.

We run both road cycling and mountain biking trips, using well-maintained bikes suited to Bhutan's terrain, with a support vehicle following to carry water, spares and tired legs when needed. Rides can be built as a single day out from Paro, Thimphu or Punakha, or strung together into a multi-day point-to-point route across two or three valleys, with comfortable hotels or homestays each night and a guide who knows exactly which side roads are worth the detour.

No racing pace here — days are paced around the ride, the views and the villages passed through, not the clock. Suitable for confident riders with reasonable fitness rather than complete beginners; let us know your experience level and we'll match the route accordingly.
