The &Beyond Punakha Escape Overview

&Beyond Punakha River Lodge sits directly on the banks of the Mo Chhu, one of the few properties in Bhutan built around a river rather than a mountainside. Part of the global &Beyond collection, it is known for opulent tented suites, exceptional personal service and a design language that puts the river at the centre of every stay.

This trip builds a full journey around it — two nights each in Paro and Thimphu on either side, with three nights at the river lodge itself to properly settle in. Days at the lodge move at a gentle pace, between riverside walks, birdwatching, gentle rafting and visits to Punakha Dzong and its famous suspension bridge.

This is a fully private, fully customisable journey — the number of nights at each stop, room category and daily activities can all be shaped around how you want to travel.

Day By Day Itinerary

Land at Paro's mountain-ringed airport and check into your hotel, with the afternoon free to explore the valley or simply unwind after your flight.

A guided hike to Tiger's Nest Monastery in the morning, then a short drive to Thimphu for the night.

A morning visiting Buddha Dordenma and Tashichho Dzong, then cross Dochula Pass and its 108 memorial chortens before checking into &Beyond Punakha River Lodge on the riverbank.

Visit Punakha Dzong, one of Bhutan's most beautiful fortresses, and walk across the valley's famous suspension bridge before returning to the lodge by the river.

A gentle rafting excursion on the Mo Chhu, with birdwatching and riverside walks for the rest of the day, and time to enjoy the lodge itself.

A final morning among Punakha's rice terraces and farmhouses, then drive back over Dochula Pass to Paro for your last night.

Transfer to Paro International Airport for your onward flight.

What Makes This Trip Different

Most Bhutan itineraries are built around mountains and monasteries — this one is built around a river. &Beyond Punakha River Lodge puts water at the centre of the stay, from tented suites that open onto the Mo Chhu to activities shaped around the riverbank itself, giving the middle of your trip a genuinely different rhythm from the rest.
